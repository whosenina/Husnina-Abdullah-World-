🫸🏻 Hello World 🫷🏻
Wonder what kind of rain and night dinasours used to see?

I love the quietness side of the world. When the sun starts changing shifts with the night and darkness slowly dominates the sky, it speaks to me like an old friend who feels excited to meet again.

I love when it rains and the water pours softly into the lake which in my mind it does look like a shining crystal sprinkle falling to give the earth some extra decorations. Much more lucky if rainbow decided to appear after the rain to steal the spotlight for a moment since rainbow rarely shown up.

“Night rain brings rainbow to me.” 🪽

What is inside that small head?
A curiousity that should be studied.

When I look at my cats, I always end up wondering what is inside their head actually? Like what are they talking about when they meet another cat or another animals such as bird, ant or dog? I do wish we have a machine or technology to understand the languages of animals so maybe we can learnt more about them. But surely I can understand very well, that meow meow before dinner is "I am hungry, feed me now".

Big shout out to my lovely cats 🐱: Sleepy, Mochi, Gebu and hero. (and many other cats that have been into my life, love you guys ❤️)

“Cats are cute, so are you"
