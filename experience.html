📽 Experiences of Miss Husnina Along Her Journey For 20 Years Old Living 🚂🚃

Entering TV program for first time

Starting from high-school experience, one day during physics class, our teacher Madam Nora told us about the upcoming Didik TV program and she will be the one in charge to do a collaboration with them. Thinking that this was a great chance for me and my friends to try new things, we decided to participate. It turned out to be such a memorable experience because we got to communicate with the host, see how they work backstage and create a rare achievement together with my high-school buddies ✨

On the flight to Sabah

I had my first flight when I was 16 to Sabah, Malaysia. Being so up high in the sky keeps on giving me a little anxiety of what if. Such as, What if the aeroplane lose it control? What if the scene in the movie happens for real? But those thoughts only stayed on the flight, once I reached the land below the wind, I am full of excited and happiness because Sabah is one of the beauty of Malaysia.

Joining Sukan Antara Fakulti (SAF)

During my second semester, I joined the badminton sports for my faculty. Little did I know, from just this one full day a mix of feeling haunted me. At first still being energetic and confident, but starting to slowly break down from tiredness, not having a proper meal and many provocation. However, I am glad that I got to exprienced a little hardness when God had a better plan. We managed to win the third place and got our own medal!
